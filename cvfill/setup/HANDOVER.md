# Auto-Apply — read me first (2 minutes)

This is your own job-application helper. You don't need to know any coding. You talk to
an assistant in plain English and it does the work.

## Install it (once)
1. Open the `cvfill` → `setup` folder.
2. **Double-click `install.command`.** Windows will pop up — just follow them.
   - It installs the assistant (Codex), signs you in with your Codex/ChatGPT account,
     builds the tool, and creates an **Auto-Apply** app.
   - If your Mac asks to install "Command Line Tools" or for your password, that's normal.
3. When it finishes, you'll have an **Auto-Apply** app in your Applications and on your Desktop.

> If double-clicking is blocked the first time, right-click `install.command` → **Open** →
> **Open**. (macOS does this for files it didn't download itself.)

## Use it (every time)
**Double-click the Auto-Apply app.** A window opens with your assistant. Just type what
you want:

- `add my resume` (it reads your PDF and saves your info)
- `find me software internships at NVIDIA and Apple`
- `log me in to my companies`
- `fill this application: ` *(paste a job link)*
- `also search Microsoft` or `add my work authorization`

The assistant runs everything for you and explains the results in plain language.

## What it does with your stuff
- Your info (name, email, resume details) is saved **only on your Mac**, in a private file.
- Any **passwords** go into your **macOS Keychain**, never into a plain file.
- Your info is only ever sent to the career site you're applying to — nowhere else.
- It **never clicks Submit**. It fills the form and stops so **you** review and submit.

## Changing how it works
You don't edit anything yourself — you just **ask the assistant**. Examples:
- "I only want jobs in Texas."
- "Also fill in my graduation date and GitHub."
- "Add my company — here's the careers page link."
- "This form didn't fill the phone number — can you fix it?"

The assistant will change the tool for you, test it, and tell you it's done. If you ever
want a fresh start, just tell it that too.

## If something seems off
Open the Auto-Apply app and describe what happened ("it couldn't find the resume",
"the NVIDIA page gave an error"). The assistant will look into it and fix it.
